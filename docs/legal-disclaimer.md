# Legal Disclaimer

**License Advisor is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.**

The software is a compliance decision-support tool for developers and teams. Outputs are generated from:

- declared project shipping intent;
- dependency metadata and lockfiles;
- license evidence the tool can observe;
- deterministic policy rules.

## Important statements

1. Findings are **alerts under stated assumptions**, not court judgments.
2. A `clear` result means no relevant obligation was detected under the current assumptions - **not** legal approval to ship.
3. A `red_flag` or `review_required` result means attention is warranted - **not** an automatic prohibition statement.
4. License metadata in ecosystems can be wrong, incomplete, or conflicting. The tool preserves conflicts when it sees them; it cannot invent certainty.
5. Some scenarios require human legal review by qualified counsel. When the engine cannot resolve a case safely, that stop is intentional.

## Recommended practice

- Keep project context accurate.
- Preserve scan reports and evidence for audit trails.
- Review material findings before release.
- Consult qualified open-source licensing counsel for high-stakes shipping decisions.
